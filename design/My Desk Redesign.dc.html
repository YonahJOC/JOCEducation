Darker sidebar: blueTint #E4E9F8 instead of white, so the nav reads as a frame and the white cards float on paper. The active page is a white row; icons sit on white tiles in their section colour.
One loud colour per screen: only the greeting band is a saturated fill. Today at JOC moves from solid orange to orangeTint, This week is greenTint, and the scratchpad is orangeTint. Tints carry the fun; nothing glares.
No pure-white field over 50% of the screen: the page is paper #FBF9F4, cards are white islands, text is ink #10233F (never pure black), and body copy is Newsreader at 16–19px.
Motion is small and rare: a 1.5s green flash on add, and the cheer after a tick. No looping animation.
Fun lives in the details: time-of-day greeting, Shabbat on the week, the tilted sticky note, Yasher koach when the day is clear.
