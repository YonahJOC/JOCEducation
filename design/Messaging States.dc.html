Turn 2 · three ways to shape it

Email, WhatsApp, or both

2a: every question is its own conversation with a subject. 2b: one running conversation per program and person. 2c: both. Messages runs like WhatsApp, and questions asked from a program page run like email. All three are live, so you can click, type and send.

2a

Like email: one conversation per question

A school picks what it's about, sees who will read it, gives it a subject, and sends. Each question can be answered and closed on its own, and there's a list of everything ever asked. The cost: a school runs into the composer as a form before they've written a word, and a quick follow-up means a new subject or digging out the old one.

2c

Both: chat in Messages, questions on the program page

Messages is the running conversation with each person (WhatsApp). A program page has its own Questions section, where each question gets a subject and its own conversation (email), and replaces today's one-shot asks. The cost: two places a reply can land. So both have to show up on Today, and a coordinator's inbox needs a tab for each.
