Turn 3 · replacing the Monday board · /admin/schools/board

Off Monday in two days

Most of what the Monday board holds is already in the portal, spread across the school record and the Status page. What the portal doesn't have is the board itself: one dense table of every school, grouped, filterable and editable in place. That's the main new screen. The rest is six new fields, versioned student lists, and a one-time import.

The CRM · JOC side

What's new

The board (3a). Every JOC App school in one table: grouped by status, network, list or owner, with sums under each group and status, sign and Network editable in the cell.

Owner. School.accountManagerId already exists; nothing sets it. Add it to the row and the drawer, plus a Mine / Nobody's filter.

New fields: JOC App status, network, middle school as a type, sign printed (dated), and student list state with the reason when it's stuck.

Student list versions (3b). Every upload is kept, newest first, instead of Monday's four file columns.

The import (3d). One time, from Monday's Excel export.

The link · both sides

Where they meet

Student list upload. A school admin uploads the list on their own portal, and it lands as the newest version on JOC's row. Marking a list stuck shows the school the reason ("Missing gender") and a button to send a fixed one.

Messages (turn 1). A conversation per program, plus General. The board shows a count of unread messages on each school.

Nothing is emailed. Both happen inside the portal, and every school still has nobody signed in, so for now JOC uploads the lists.

The school portal

Unchanged, plus one panel

"Your student list" on Today, for a school admin: whether JOC has a usable list, why not if not, and an upload button. Teachers never see it; it holds students' names.

Owner, network, the sign and the App status stay internal. A school never sees "Off track".
