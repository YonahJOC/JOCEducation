E · Buttons and hit areas

Boxed buttons are 44 tall. Small controls stretch to fill their row.

Two rules. A button with a visible box is never shorter than 44px, because a box that looks 30px but taps 44 overlaps its neighbour. A control without a box (Today, Nudge, ×, the tick) has its hit area grown by the row, not by negative margins into the row above. The row gets minHeight: 44, and each control alignSelf: stretch with margin: −8px 0 to cancel the row's 8px padding. Shaded areas below are the real hit areas.
